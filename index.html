MAIA Personal

MAIA Personal es un asistente privado que usa una sola persona, Alex, su propietario. Funciona en un ordenador suyo y, con su permiso expreso por OAuth, accede a sus propias cuentas de Google (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive y Contactos) para prepararle resúmenes, recordatorios y avisos.

No es un servicio público. No admite registros ni usuarios de terceros.

English. MAIA Personal is a private assistant used by a single person, its owner Alex. It runs on his own computer and, with his explicit OAuth consent, accesses his own Google accounts (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Contacts) to prepare personal summaries, reminders and notifications. It is not a public service and does not accept third-party users.
